# Carrier PCB

__One two-sided board on the PayloadSled's centre line__ (operator, 2026-09-11). The __tall side__ faces 270°, the camera port, and carries both XIAO stacks. The __low side__ faces 90°, the web, and carries the sensors. The board hangs off the web on two M3 standoffs, so it comes off the sled as one unit. How the design was settled is in [PCB-carrier-design.md](PCB-carrier-design.md).

The sled side of the interface is [js-rocket#99](https://github.com/jwilleke/js-rocket/issues/99). This page is what the board is and what is still missing from it.

| | |
|---|---|
| Size | __24 × 90.4 mm__, nose z 25.4 → 115.8 |
| Stackup | __4-layer__, solid ground plane |
| Thickness | __1.0 mm FR4__ |
| Nets | __9__: +3V3, +3V3_LORA, GND, VBAT, SDA, SCL, BUZZER, GPS_TX, GPS_RX |
| Mounting | two __surface-mount M3 standoffs__, 10 mm (Würth WA-SMSI 9774100360), on the low side. __M3 plastic screws, M3 × 10__, from the web's far face |

__The sensors run lengthwise, not turned.__ Their pin rows then sit __2.15 mm inboard of the tall-side modules' rows__, parallel, so no hole ever lands on another. Turned 90°, their rows would cross the modules' rows. They stand __~1 mm proud of the board on their pins__, header plastic up against the sensor, so the plastic clears the tall side's solder joints on the low face.

__The battery keeps its position.__ It sits forward of the board, where [payload-sled.md](https://github.com/jwilleke/js-rocket/blob/main/docs/3d-printed-parts/payload-sled.md#where-the-cell-goes) has it, with ~9 mm to spare.

## Three voltages, and only one of them is on this board

| Rail | Volts | Where it comes from | On the carrier? |
|---|---|---|---|
| __USB / VBUS__ | __5.0__ | A USB-C lead, only while one is plugged in | __No__ — it exists on each XIAO's `5V` pin (pin 14) and nowhere else. On battery power there is no voltage on it |
| __Battery__ | __3.7 nominal__ | The [LiPo](../LiPo-500mAh/LiPo-500mAh.md), to the JST, then by soldered pigtail to each XIAO's underside __BAT pads__ | As `VBAT`, between the JST and the wire pads. It does __not__ reach the headers |
| __Logic__ | __3.3__ | Each XIAO's own regulator | __Yes__ — see below |

__One regulator per load.__ XIAO-ESP32S3-cam's `3V3` feeds the +3V3 plane and the sensors. XIAO-ESP32S3-lora's `3V3` feeds only the L76K-GNSS, on its own net, `+3V3_LORA`, __kept off the plane__ — so no two regulators are ever paralleled. The carrier carries no regulator of its own.

```text
  LiPo 3.7 V
     |  lead from the forward end
     v
  JST-PH -- VBAT/GND --+-- pigtail --> XIAO-ESP32S3-cam  BAT pads
                       +-- pigtail --> XIAO-ESP32S3-lora BAT pads
  XIAO-ESP32S3-cam  3V3 --> +3V3 plane --> LSM6DSO32, BMP388 (BMP388 CS tied high: I2C, #19)
  XIAO-ESP32S3-lora 3V3 --> +3V3_LORA  --> L76K-GNSS
```

__JST pin 1 is `VBAT` in the generator, and it must be checked against the battery's red lead before soldering.__ JST-PH polarity is not standardised, and reversing a LiPo into a XIAO destroys it. The silkscreen marks `+` at the JST and at each `VBAT` wire pad.

__Everything else is powered by the XIAO it plugs onto__, through that module's B2B connector: the [Wio-SX1262](../Wio-SX1262-LoRa/Wio-SX1262-LoRa.md) and the [Sense-camera-board](../Sense-camera-board/Sense-camera-board.md) with its camera and [microSD](../microSD/microSD.md). The [PS1240 buzzer](../PS1240-buzzer/PS1240-buzzer.md) is not on the board: it sits against the nose wall on two leads from the `BUZZER` and GND wire pads. The antennas are on the sled ([Antennas.md](../Antennas/Antennas.md)), so __no RF crosses the carrier__.

### Charge through the Sense stack, and only that one

__Operator, 2026-09-08.__ For charging alone it makes no difference — both XIAOs have a charger and both sit on the same battery. __The decision is about which port is permanently committed__, because [#1](https://github.com/jwilleke/js-rocket-avionics/issues/1)'s service pigtail occupies whichever one it is wired to:

- __[XIAO-ESP32S3-cam](../XIAO-ESP32S3-cam/XIAO-ESP32S3-cam.md), the Sense stack__ — the only one that reaches the microSD, and the one being reflashed constantly, since its firmware does not exist yet
- __[XIAO-ESP32S3-lora](../XIAO-ESP32S3-lora/XIAO-ESP32S3-lora.md)__ — runs __stock Meshtastic and must never be reflashed__. That is the whole reason there are two modules. A service port on it invites exactly what the two-module split exists to prevent

__So XIAO-ESP32S3-lora's USB-C is not used in the flight build.__ __And still one at a time__ — [Two chargers on one battery](../LiPo-500mAh/LiPo-500mAh.md#two-chargers-on-one-battery) is why.

## Build rules

- __4-layer, solid ground plane.__ A few dollars more at this size; fixes return paths and coupling from the camera's DVP flex
- __Module footprints, not bare chips.__ A bare LSM6DSO32 is an LGA-14 at 2.5 × 3 mm and is not hand-solderable. Soldering breakouts down still gives one rigid assembly — apart from the battery pigtails, which are unavoidable
- __Solder the headers — no loose sockets.__ Conformal coat after bench testing
- __Assembly order: the low side first.__ Solder the sensors, standing ~1 mm proud, and trim their tails on the tall face, then the tall side's modules. A sensor joint on the tall face sits right beside a module header's plastic
- __The screws stop inside the standoffs.__ Each standoff has a 4.4 mm hole through the board, and standoff 1's opens under XIAO-ESP32S3-cam: __M3 × 10__ through the 3 mm web leaves 3 mm short of the board. A longer screw reaches the XIAO's back
- __The battery pigtails are anchored to the board, not held by their solder joints.__ Wire spec and anchor are in [LiPo-500mAh.md](../LiPo-500mAh/LiPo-500mAh.md#the-pigtail-is-the-fragile-part-and-the-solder-joint-must-not-be-the-anchor), which owns them
- __Mark polarity__ at the JST and the `VBAT` pads

## Where it actually is

| Stage | State |
|---|---|
| 2a — outline, standoffs, stackup | __done__ |
| 2b — nets and footprints | __done__ — every footprint placed and netted |
| 2c — placement | __done in the generator__, pending the operator's review of the drawing |
| 2d — routing | __not started__. DRC reports __0 violations__ and 10 unconnected items, which are exactly the unrouted signal nets. Autorouting is wrong here — [#15](https://github.com/jwilleke/js-rocket-avionics/issues/15) |
| 2e — gerber + drill export | chain proven; needs a finished board |

__The one DRC exception__ is in `PCB-carrier.kicad_dru`, which the generator writes: Würth's standoff land pattern puts its pads right at the edge of its own hole, which is the maker's design. It is allowed for the two standoffs and nowhere else.

__Checks owed before fabrication__ — [PCB-carrier-design.md](PCB-carrier-design.md#checks-owed-before-building) keeps the list: the standoff part in stock, JST pin 1 against the battery, and Meshtastic's GPS pins (#6).

> __`fab/gerbers/` holds a complete-looking set from an old board.__ It is the single most likely way a wrong board gets ordered — it is complete, it is in the repo, and it looks done. __Regenerate, never reuse__ — [#16](https://github.com/jwilleke/js-rocket-avionics/issues/16).

## Read the board, not the script

An earlier generator revision put GPS on pins 6/7 and I2C on 4/5 — __D5/D6 and D3/D4, every one off by one__. It was caught by __reading the mapping back out of the saved board__, not by trusting the script's output. The generator now names pins (`XIAO_PIN["D6"]`) so the numbers never appear by hand.

> __It happened again, twice.__ [#18](https://github.com/jwilleke/js-rocket-avionics/issues/18): the whole `+8.5 mm` row mirrored end for end, `+3V3` and `GND` on __D9 and D8__. [#21](https://github.com/jwilleke/js-rocket-avionics/issues/21): a back-mounted XIAO netted as if on the front, `BUZZER` on the __5V pin__.
>
> __Now the checks run, from two sources each.__ `verify_pins()` holds each XIAO's footprint table against the underside photograph, the L76K-GNSS's turned Seeed drawing against the same drawing turned by eye, and each sensor — on the back, where KiCad's top view mirrors it — against its front photograph carried there by two independent routes. `verify_clearances()` checks every through-hole against every other across both sides, and against the standoffs and the BMP388's legs. Putting past bugs back fails the build: a mirrored L76K-GNSS, a sensor placed without the back-side mirror, and a sensor pin moved onto a module pin are all caught.

## The KiCad sources are in this folder

`PCB-carrier.kicad_pcb`, `PCB-carrier.kicad_pro` and `PCB-carrier.kicad_dru` sit beside this page, with `PCB-carrier-layout.json`, which the diagram reads. The generator writes the board, the rules and the layout; the CLI recipes in the [repo README](../../README.md) read from the board. `.kicad_prl` is local editor state and is gitignored.

## Ordering

__Do not order copper before breadboarding.__ A layout error costs ~$33 and __two weeks__; a wiring error costs minutes. The gate is [#4](https://github.com/jwilleke/js-rocket-avionics/issues/4); the ordering epic is [#11](https://github.com/jwilleke/js-rocket-avionics/issues/11). Three copies from OSH Park: one to fly, two spares.

__The two standoffs are the sled interface.__ [js-rocket#99](https://github.com/jwilleke/js-rocket/issues/99) drills the web at their positions, so moving either changes the sled.
