# Carrier PCB

__One two-sided board on the PayloadSled's centre line.__ The __tall side__ faces 270°, the camera port, and carries both XIAO stacks. The __low side__ faces 90°, the web, and carries the sensors. The board hangs off the web on two M3 standoffs, so it comes off the sled as one unit.

__This page is the carrier's one source of truth, and it describes the current design.__ The layout drawing, the fit mock and the [nose-assembly drawing](https://github.com/jwilleke/js-rocket/blob/main/docs/designs/nose-assembly-6e81cfc.html) must all show what this page says. [PCB-carrier-design.md](PCB-carrier-design.md) holds the reasoning behind the choices; where it gives a different number, this page wins. Decisions and their dates live on the GitHub issues linked below.

The sled side of the interface is [js-rocket#99](https://github.com/jwilleke/js-rocket/issues/99). __Board x maps to the sled mirrored: sled x = 12 − board x.__ The tall side is KiCad's front and faces 270°, so seen from there with forward up, the board's +x points to the sled's −x. Confirmed on the printed fit mock and ElectronicsSled.

| | |
|---|---|
| Size | __24 × 115.7 mm__, nose z 25.4 → 141.1 |
| Stackup | __4-layer__, solid ground plane |
| Thickness | __1.6 mm FR4__, OSH Park's only 4-layer thickness. Low face at r 0.5 — [why](../../README.md#the-interface-to-the-sled) |
| Nets | +3V3, +3V3_LORA, GND, VBAT, SDA, SCL, BUZZER, GPS_TX, GPS_RX. the [charge circuit](../charge-circuit/charge-circuit.md) adds the cam XIAO's `5V` |
| Mounting | two __surface-mount M3 standoffs__, 10 mm (Würth WA-SMSI 9774100360), on the low side. __M3 plastic screws, M3 × 10__, from the web's far face |

__The sensors run lengthwise, not turned.__ Turned 90°, a sensor's rows would cross the modules' rows. The BMP388 sits behind no module. The LSM6DSO32 sits behind XIAO-ESP32S3-cam, and its rows, 12.7 mm apart, cannot fit inside the cam's 15.24. So it is slid sideways until one row is outside: its holes sit 2.18 mm from the cam's, and its joints clear the cam's header plastic. The sensors stand __~1 mm proud of the board on their pins__, header plastic up against the sensor, so the plastic clears the tall side's solder joints on the low face.

__The battery sits behind the board__, between its low face and the web, with a cage's forward wall clear of the BMP388. __This page sets its position.__ `BATTERY_Y` in `gen_carrier.py` copies it for the drawing and the mock, and the sled's cage is built to it. What is still owed around it: [PCB-carrier-design.md](PCB-carrier-design.md#the-battery-sits-behind-the-carrier--decided-2026-09-14-layout-b2-2026-09-15).

## Power

| Rail | Volts | Where it comes from | On the carrier? |
|---|---|---|---|
| __USB__ | __5.0__ | A USB-C lead, only while one is plugged in | __From XIAO-ESP32S3-cam's `5V` pin only__: it feeds the charger chip. XIAO-ESP32S3-lora's `5V` pin is not connected. On battery power there is no voltage on it |
| __Battery__ | __3.7 nominal__ | The [LiPo](../LiPo-500mAh/LiPo-500mAh.md), to the JST, then by soldered pigtail to each XIAO's underside __BAT pads__ | As `VBAT`, between the JST, the charging circuit and the wire pads. It does __not__ reach the headers |
| __Logic__ | __3.3__ | Each XIAO's own regulator | __Yes__ — see below |

__One regulator per load.__ XIAO-ESP32S3-cam's `3V3` feeds the +3V3 plane and the sensors. XIAO-ESP32S3-lora's `3V3` feeds only the L76K-GNSS, on its own net, `+3V3_LORA`, __kept off the plane__, so no two regulators are ever paralleled. The carrier has no regulator of its own.

```text
  LiPo 3.7 V
     |  lead from the forward end
     v
  JST-PH -- VBAT/GND --+-- pigtail ---------------------------> XIAO-ESP32S3-cam  BAT pads
                       +-- pigtail ---------------------------> XIAO-ESP32S3-lora BAT pads
                       +-- charger chip <-- XIAO-ESP32S3-cam 5V pin (USB only)
  XIAO-ESP32S3-cam  3V3 --> +3V3 plane --> LSM6DSO32, BMP388 (BMP388 CS tied high: I2C, #19)
  XIAO-ESP32S3-lora 3V3 --> +3V3_LORA  --> L76K-GNSS
```

__JST pin 1 is `VBAT` in the generator, and it must be checked against the battery's red lead before soldering.__ JST-PH polarity is not standardised, and reversing a LiPo into a XIAO destroys it. The silkscreen marks `+` at the JST and at each `VBAT` wire pad.

__Everything else is powered by the XIAO it plugs onto__, through that module's B2B connector: the [Wio-SX1262](../Wio-SX1262-LoRa/Wio-SX1262-LoRa.md) and the [Sense-camera-board](../Sense-camera-board/Sense-camera-board.md) with its camera and [microSD](../microSD/microSD.md). The [PS1240 buzzer](../PS1240-buzzer/PS1240-buzzer.md) is not on the board: it sits against the nose wall on two leads from the `BUZZER` and GND wire pads. The antennas are on the sled ([Antennas.md](../Antennas/Antennas.md)), so __no RF crosses the carrier__.

## Charging

__The charge circuit is its own page: [charge-circuit.md](../charge-circuit/charge-circuit.md)__ — what it is, how it behaves, and its values. On this board it is the charger chip's adapter standing on __six through-holes__ in the tall side's forward end, with its resistor and two capacitors beside it, fed from XIAO-ESP32S3-cam's `5V` pin.

### Charge through the Sense stack, and only that one

__XIAO-ESP32S3-cam's USB-C is the service port.__ It is the Sense stack, the only one that reaches the microSD, and the one reflashed most. [#1](https://github.com/jwilleke/js-rocket-avionics/issues/1)'s service pigtail is wired to it, and the charging circuit is fed from it.

__XIAO-ESP32S3-lora's USB-C is for flashing and testing on the bench.__ It runs [stock Meshtastic and nothing else](../XIAO-ESP32S3-lora/XIAO-ESP32S3-lora.md#firmware--stock-meshtastic-only-updated-on-purpose-frozen-for-flight), and nothing on the carrier is fed from it. Plugged in, it runs from its own USB and beacons as normal. Turned 90°, its port is reachable with the module soldered down.

__USB into one XIAO at a time, never both__ — [charge-circuit.md](../charge-circuit/charge-circuit.md#how-it-behaves).

## Build rules

- __4-layer, solid ground plane.__ A few dollars more at this size; fixes return paths and coupling from the camera's DVP flex
- __Module footprints, not bare chips.__ A bare LSM6DSO32 is an LGA-14 at 2.5 × 3 mm and is not hand-solderable, and the charger chip goes on its adapter rather than onto the carrier
- __Solder the headers — no loose sockets.__ Conformal coat after bench testing
- __Assembly order: the low side first.__ Solder the sensors, standing ~1 mm proud, and trim their tails on the tall face, then the tall side's modules. A sensor joint on the tall face sits right beside a module header's plastic
- __The screws stop inside the standoffs.__ Each standoff has a 4.4 mm hole through the board, so neither sits under a module. __M3 × 10__ through the 3 mm web leaves 3 mm short of the board; a longer one pokes into the USB-C plug room
- __The battery pigtails are anchored to the board, not held by their solder joints.__ Wire spec and anchor are in [LiPo-500mAh.md](../LiPo-500mAh/LiPo-500mAh.md#the-pigtail-is-the-fragile-part-and-the-solder-joint-must-not-be-the-anchor), which owns them
- __Mark polarity__ at the JST and the `VBAT` pads

## Where it actually is

| Stage | State |
|---|---|

| 2b — nets and footprints | __done for what the generator draws__, not for [Not yet in the generator](#not-yet-in-the-generator) |

| 2e — gerber + drill export | chain proven; needs a finished board |

__The one DRC exception__ is in `PCB-carrier.kicad_dru`, which the generator writes: Würth's standoff land pattern puts its pads right at the edge of its own hole, which is the maker's design. It is allowed for the two standoffs and nowhere else.

__Checks owed before fabrication__ — [PCB-carrier-design.md](PCB-carrier-design.md#checks-owed-before-building) keeps the list: the standoff part in stock and JST pin 1 against the battery. Meshtastic's GPS pins are D6/D7, read off the board (#6).

> __`fab/gerbers/` holds a complete-looking set from an old board.__ It is the single most likely way a wrong board gets ordered — it is complete, it is in the repo, and it looks done. __Regenerate, never reuse__ — [#16](https://github.com/jwilleke/js-rocket-avionics/issues/16).

## Read the board, not the script

__Pin mappings are checked against the saved board, from two sources each.__ The generator names pins (`XIAO_PIN["D6"]`), so pin numbers never appear by hand. `verify_pins()` holds each XIAO's footprint table against the underside photograph, the L76K-GNSS's turned Seeed drawing against the same drawing turned by eye, and each sensor — on the back, where KiCad's top view mirrors it — against its front photograph carried there by two independent routes. `verify_clearances()` checks every through-hole against every other across both sides, and against the standoffs and the BMP388's legs. Each past pin bug ([#18](https://github.com/jwilleke/js-rocket-avionics/issues/18), [#21](https://github.com/jwilleke/js-rocket-avionics/issues/21)) fails the build if put back.

## The KiCad sources are in this folder

`PCB-carrier.kicad_pcb`, `PCB-carrier.kicad_pro` and `PCB-carrier.kicad_dru` sit beside this page, with `PCB-carrier-layout.json`, which the diagram reads. The generator writes the board, the rules and the layout; the CLI recipes in the [repo README](../../README.md) read from the board. `.kicad_prl` is local editor state and is gitignored.

## Fit mock — print it before ordering copper

__[`fit-mock/PCB-carrier-fit-mock.stl`](fit-mock/PCB-carrier-fit-mock.stl)__ — the generated board's own body, exported by KiCad, so it cannot drift from the generator. Regenerate it after any board change with [`gen_carrier_fitmock.py`](../scripts/gen_carrier_fitmock.py); do not edit it. __Do not print it while [Not yet in the generator](#not-yet-in-the-generator) lists anything.__ It is a copy with three changes, never the board:

- __Every hole opened 0.3 mm__ on diameter, because printed holes come out small: header holes Ø1.3 (Ø1.0 on the real board), standoff holes Ø4.7 (4.4)
- __An arrow cut through the board, pointing forward__ (toward the nose tip) at the forward end, beside the forward standoff. Printed low side down, the top face is the tall side
- __Four small marker holes at the edges__ where the battery cavity's two ends fall, nose z 67.9 and 105.3, read from `PCB-carrier-layout.json`

__1.6 mm thick__, like the real board. KiCad exports the core alone at 1.51 mm, so the script scales it, which puts the header plastic at its true height.

__No labels on it.__ The slicer drops silkscreen-sized strokes. __What goes where is on paper__ — [`PCB-carrier-template.pdf`](PCB-carrier-template.pdf), both sides at full size, generated by [`gen_carrier_template.py`](../scripts/gen_carrier_template.py) from the layout and the mock's own holes. Print it at 100 % and check its 100 mm bar with a ruler.

__Print:__ PETG HF, flat, __low side down__ (the STL has it at z 0), 0.2 mm layers, 2 walls, no support, no brim. About 5 g and 23 minutes. __Before sending a plate, prove it in the G-code__: every hole open on every layer, the height 1.6 mm, and the first layer on the bed.

__What it checks, with the bench parts:__

1. __Both XIAO stacks and the L76K-GNSS push onto their holes__ without forcing
2. __Both XIAOs' USB-C ports take a plug__, and the L76K-GNSS antenna cable fits without touching the lora stack
3. __The sensors on the low side__, if their headers are fitted, land in their own holes, clear of the tall side's pins
4. __The whole populated mock passes a 40 mm bore__
5. __The battery between its marks__, held 5.25 mm off the low side as the cage holds it: its lead reaches the JST around the edge
6. __Screw it through the standoff holes__, to spacers or the sled, and see how the span behaves. PETG is roughly ten times more flexible than FR4, so a mock that holds its shape says the real board will; a mock that sags says nothing either way

If the pins will not pass, open the holes with a 1.2–1.3 mm drill, or regenerate with a larger `HOLE_GROW`.

## Ordering

__Do not order copper before breadboarding.__ A layout error costs ~$43 and __two weeks__; a wiring error costs minutes. The gate is [#4](https://github.com/jwilleke/js-rocket-avionics/issues/4); the ordering epic is [#11](https://github.com/jwilleke/js-rocket-avionics/issues/11). Three copies from OSH Park: one to fly, two spares.

__The two standoffs are the sled interface.__ [js-rocket#99](https://github.com/jwilleke/js-rocket/issues/99) drills the web at their positions, so moving either changes the sled.
